# Local adapter mesh

`adapter_flange.stl` models the black spacer between the FR3 wrist and the
Inspire hand palm. Its compact binary STL is a closed 32-sided cylinder, 76 mm
in diameter and 10 mm thick, centred on its mesh origin. The URDF places its
centre 5 mm above `fr3_link8`, while the palm mount is translated 10 mm along
the same axis.
